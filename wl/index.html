How Water Lock Works

Water Lock is designed to turn everyday phone habits into healthy reminders. Instead of reaching straight for distracting apps, you get a simple prompt to hydrate first.

1. Choose which apps to pause

Select the apps you use most often — social media, games, or anything that tends to keep you scrolling.

2. Get a reminder to drink water

When you try to open a locked app, Water Lock reminds you to pause and drink before continuing.

3. Drink, then unlock

Once you’ve taken that moment to hydrate, the app is released and you can continue with your day.

Why Water Lock

💧 Build a hydration habit

Small daily reminders can help you drink more water naturally.

📱 Turn screen time into a healthy pause

Instead of mindless opening, Water Lock adds a simple moment of care.

🔔 Gentle reminders, not pressure

The experience is designed to feel light, helpful, and easy to follow.

🌿 Simple and intentional

Water Lock helps you be more mindful of both your phone habits and your health.
