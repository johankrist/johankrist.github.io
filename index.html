What We Build

ATP Creative is a home for simple app ideas that turn everyday phone habits into healthier, more mindful routines. Our focus is on tools that gently encourage better habits through intentional pauses and practical reminders.

We believe technology should not only capture attention, but also help people pause, reflect, and build habits that support a better life.
