How Meditate Lock Works

Meditate Lock turns everyday phone use into a mindful pause. Before opening certain apps, you’re invited to slow down, breathe, and take a moment to reset.

1. Choose which apps to pause

Select the apps that usually pull you into distraction — social media, games, or anything you want to use more intentionally.

2. Get a reminder to meditate first

When you try to open a paused app, Meditate Lock reminds you to take a short moment to meditate before continuing.

3. Meditate, then unlock

After your meditation moment, the app is released so you can continue with a clearer, calmer mind.

Why Meditate Lock

🧘 Build a meditation habit

Create a simple daily practice by linking phone habits with mindful pauses.

📱 Turn distraction into reset

Instead of jumping straight into apps, take a brief moment to breathe and refocus.

🔔 Gentle reminders, not pressure

The experience is designed to feel calm, supportive, and easy to follow.
